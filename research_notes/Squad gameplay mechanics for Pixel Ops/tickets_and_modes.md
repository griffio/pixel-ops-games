## Key question 1: Ticket pools, ticket costs and ticket bleed (starting tickets; infantry death, revive, give-up, bleed-out; vehicle classes; FOB radio/HAB; flag capture/loss; bleed)

### Takeaway
Current Squad sizes pools by mode, not by map: AAS/RAAS 250 per side (200 on a handful of small maps), Invasion 250 attackers vs 800 defenders, Insurgency/Destruction 200 attackers vs 1000 defenders, Territory Control 400, Skirmish 200, Seed 100. Losses are small and fixed: 1 ticket per infantry death (only once they bleed out or give up; a revive costs nothing), 2 per commander, 20 per FOB radio, 1 to 15 per vehicle (MBT 15 is the top). The big swings come from objectives: +50 for taking an enemy flag, +20 for a fresh neutral one, 0 for losing a flag (since March 2023), +125 per Invasion capture, plus mode-specific bleeds and a 60-tickets-per-minute "mercy bleed" once one side holds every flag.

#### Ticket bleed
- AAS bleed (wiki): you start bleeding the enemy once you hold "at least one objective on the enemy side of the flag lattice… the enemy will lose 1 ticket per minute" per such objective. On odd-count layers the central flag is "middle ground" and triggers no bleed. On even-count layers you need "more than half". "In the case of 'double-neutral' stalemate… all ticket bleed mechanics will be paused" — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure)
- Mercy bleed (wiki): once one team holds all control points, the other bleeds "60 Tickets over 60 seconds (the tickets tick down continuously every second)", stopping if they retake a point — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure). Patch 8.2 (2024-12-02) "AAS v1 - Fixed mercy bleed instantly ending the round after last capture point taken", which confirms AAS mercy bleed still exists — [Patch 8.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1784506359151949)
- RAAS: Patch 4.3 (2023-03-14) "Removed Ticket Bleed from RAAS layers on all flags except when all flags have been captured (which still has -60 tickets per minute mercy bleed)" — [Patch 4.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5070528810311080632); also [Wiki: RAAS (edited 2024-09-22)](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Advance_and_Secure)
- Invasion: "There is no ticket bleed for either Defender or Attacker" — [Wiki: Invasion](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion)
- Skirmish (since 9.0): "The team that controls the majority of capture points will cause a ticket bleed on the opposing team. The ticket bleed increases with each additional capture point controlled"; capturing the enemy anchor "will trigger a mercy bleed" — [Patch 9.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1809235871756104)
- Territory Control bleed curve (per minute, by share of hexes the enemy holds): 1 at 55–59%, 2 at 60–65%, 3 at 66–69%, 4 at 70–74%, 5 at 75–79%, 6 at 80–84%, 30 at 85–89%, 120 at 90–100% — [Wiki: Territory Control (edited 2024-06-10; content older)](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Territory_Control). ### Inferences
- Approximate current vehicle table for a designer: motorbike/quad 1, boat (RHIB) 1, transport truck 3, logistics truck 5, light vehicle/MRAP/technical 5, light APC 5, heavier APC 10, IFV 10, MGS 10, AT light vehicle 10, MBT 15, helicopter 5 (transport and Loach). I pieced this together from the sources above. The helicopter, artillery and boat values rest on wiki pages, not recent patch notes.
- In Squad the infantry ticket is effectively charged when the player respawns (give-up or timer run-out). A 300 s revive window means medics save most tickets, so infantry tickets matter far less than objectives: one enemy-flag capture (+50) equals 50 infantry deaths.
- Objective swings dominate. With 250-ticket pools, one +50 capture is 20% of a pool, and mercy bleed (60/min) ends a match within about 4 minutes of a team losing every flag.
- **Pixel Ops: differs.** Pools are 500 everywhere (`rules.js`), versus Squad's 250 for AAS. Pixel Ops Invasion gives attackers MORE tickets (650 vs 400) and Destruction 600 vs 450, the reverse of Squad (attackers 250 vs defenders 800/1000).
- **Pixel Ops: already have (shorter window).** 1 ticket per infantry respawn and a revive saves it, the same rule as Squad. Squad's revive window is up to 300 s against Pixel Ops' 12 s, and Squad charges the commander 2.
- **Pixel Ops: differs (scale and order).** Truck 10, supply truck 16, APC 20, helicopter 30 run 2–6× Squad's values (3, 5, 5–10, 5). Squad's helicopters are among its cheapest losses (5), while Pixel Ops makes them the most expensive. Squad's priciest loss is the MBT at 15.
- **Pixel Ops: differs.** A FOB costs 40 against Squad's radio 20. Squad adds a 75 s bleed-out rebuild window and makes a friendly teardown free; check whether Pixel Ops has either.
- **Pixel Ops: differs.** A lost location costs the loser 20 in Pixel Ops. Squad has charged nothing for a lost flag since 4.3 and instead pays the capturer +50 (enemy flag) or +20 (first-time neutral). Pixel Ops has no capture reward except an optional `doctrine.captureBonus`.
- **Pixel Ops: differs (much faster).** Holding the majority bleeds the enemy 1 ticket per 5 s, i.e. 12/min flat. Squad's AAS bleed is 1/min per flag held past the midpoint (so a 5-flag map peaks at about 2/min before mercy), RAAS has no bleed until mercy, and mercy is 60/min. Pixel Ops lacks a mercy-bleed tier and lacks the "middle flag is neutral ground" exemption.

### Gaps
- No primary source gives current per-vehicle tickets for helicopters (transport/attack), artillery trucks, or emplacements. The wiki generic table says helicopters cost 5, but it was last edited 2024-12-05. I found no source saying emplacements or the HAB cost tickets.
- The Patch 10.2 line "Updated Transport ticket costs from 5 to 3" does not say whether it covers only transport trucks or also transport helicopters and boats.
- The AAS per-flag bleed rule (1/min per flag past the midpoint) comes from the wiki (2023 edit). It is consistent with patch notes (only RAAS bleed was removed) but has not been restated in any 2024–2026 patch notes.
- Skirmish bleed rates per extra point and the Skirmish mercy-bleed rate (9.0 rework) were not published in any source I found.

## Key question 2: Advance and Secure (AAS) structure and capture mechanics

### Takeaway
AAS is a fixed linear chain ("lattice") of 5–8 flags between two uncapturable main bases. Each team can attack only the next enemy or neutral flag adjacent to its own front and can lose only its own front flag. Capturing an enemy flag means draining it to neutral, then filling it to your colour. Capture speed scales with the attacker's head-count advantage up to 27 players (77 s for an initial capture at 9 players). A team wins when the enemy's tickets reach 0, typically after a full sweep triggers the 60/min mercy bleed.

### Cited Findings
- Layer data 10.0 flag counts for AAS (excluding the two mains): 5 flags on 22 layers, 6 on 6, 7 on 16, 8 on 2 — [Layer data 10.0](https://github.com/Squad-Wiki/squad-wiki-pipeline-map-data/blob/dev/completed_output/_Current%20Version/finished.json)
- Order and fronts: "Control Points can only be captured in a specific order… Your team cannot capture a Control Point out of order, your team will need to capture and hold the previous flag. This is indicated by the Attack and Defense markers." "If both teams neutralize two opposing flags in the CP's line of sequence, it's a stalemate - neither team can take their next attack flag. Only after the defense flag has been re-captured, can the attack flag be captured next." Teams start from main bases, "or in certain circumstance a limited forward spawn area" — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure)
- Map icons distinguish "CP is captured by your faction and can be attacked by the other team" (defend), "CP is captured by the other team and can be attacked by your faction" (attack), CPs that "can't be attacked at the moment", and main bases that "can't be captured" — [Wiki: Control Point (edited 2024-06-10)](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Control_Point)
- Neutralise then capture: the HUD shows an enemy-held CP's bar running down "till neutral", then "CP is captured from neutral… A progress bar… shows the cap status till captured" — [Wiki: Control Point](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Control_Point)
- Player requirements and contest: "Neutral CPs can be capped by a single player. If only one team is able to capture a control point such as during the beginning of the round or during a double neutral, it's not possible for enemies to block the capping process (ex. 1 player can cap against 9 players). On points where both teams can cap a point, blocking is possible as cap speed is based on difference in numbers between players (ex. 8v0 caps faster than 8v4). If capturing a Non-Neutral CP, teams will require at least three more players on the point. Cap speed also varies by gamemode" — [Wiki: Control Point](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Control_Point)
- The minimum-player count is a per-layer setting: Patch 2.11 fixed Skirmish layers "with the incorrect setting for minimum # soldiers to capture a flag (was 3, now 2) and incorrect flag capture time (was 120sec, now 90sec)" — [Patch 2.11](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4022264629308405733)
- Capture speed scaling introduced in Patch 2.12 (2022-02-09): "capture speed will be shortened if one team has significantly more players in the point than the other team… especially during the early stages in a round and during 'Double Neutral' scenarios" — [Patch 2.12](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4235075565593511531)
- Patch 2.15 (2022-04-12) infographic "Capture times by player advantage" (seconds to fill the capture bar, by the capturing team's head-count advantage of +1…+8). AAS/RAAS/Invasion v2.15: 220, 200, 180, 165, 150, 135, 120, 100 (v2.14 was a flat 120 s for +1 to +3, then 109, 100, 92, 86, 80). Skirmish v2.15: 100, 95, 90, 82, 75, 69, 64, 60. Stated intent: "incentivizing capturing points en masse… discourage rushing flags… rather than a lone enemy on back-cap duty" — [Patch 2.15](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4353300134244517023) (infographic image embedded in the notes)

- Capture-zone sizes (my tally of sphere volumes in layer data 10.0; a zone can be built from several spheres, boxes or capsules): AAS median sphere radius 70 m (10th–90th percentile 37–119 m); RAAS 70 m (40–107 m); Invasion 80 m (50–120 m); Skirmish 42 m (29–58 m); Seed 40 m; capsules about 40 m radius — [Layer data 10.0](https://github.com/Squad-Wiki/squad-wiki-pipeline-map-data/blob/dev/completed_output/_Current%20Version/finished.json)
- Ticket results of capture: +50 enemy flag, +20 never-owned neutral flag, 0 for the loser (see Key question 1) — [Patch 9.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1809235871756104); [Patch 4.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5070528810311080632)
- Staging: "Some game modes have a 3-minute 'Staging Period'… unable to leave the Main Base… unable to start vehicle engines… cannot fire" — [Wiki: Game Modes](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Modes). Patch 10.3 (2026-02-17) added the server setting "PrepTimeStandard… (Staging duration). This doesn't affect Invasion, Insurgency, Destruction, or Skirmish. Limit 60-300(s)" for AAS, RAAS and TC, plus "PrepTimeSmallScale" (60–300 s) for Skirmish — [Patch 10.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1824644522848347)
- Win: "The team who runs out of tickets first loses"; "individual scores do not affect the result" — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure)
- OWI said in Sept 2026 it will be "looking into reworking game modes such as AAS/RAAS and Insurgency" — [CDT Progress Report (2026-09-28)](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1844751498232628)

### Inferences
- Under the 8.1 numbers, capturing an enemy-held flag needs two bar fills (neutralise, then capture). My estimate is about 150 s with 9 uncontested attackers. Squad does not publish this figure; I derived it from "77 seconds for an initial flag capture".
- The 2.15 curve (100 s at +8) and the 8.1 figure (77 s at 9 players "same as the previous maximum") disagree, so the curve was retuned at an undocumented point between April 2022 and Sept 2024. Treat the 2.15 numbers as historical shape only.
- **Pixel Ops: already have (core).** Five fixed locations captured in lattice order with neutralise-then-capture progress (-100..+100) match Squad's AAS core. The double-neutral rule falls out of `capturable()` requiring the previous location (`objectives.js`).
- **Pixel Ops: differs (contest rule).** In Pixel Ops any enemy unit freezes progress (`(n0 && n1)` skips the tick). Squad caps by head-count difference when both sides can capture, and needs at least 3 players (per the wiki) to attack a held flag.
- **Pixel Ops: differs (speed).** Progress is 1.2 × n per second, capped at n = 8: a neutral capture takes about 21 s with 4 soldiers and about 10 s with 8, and an enemy-held one double that. Squad scales to 27 players and is roughly 4–8× slower in absolute time (77 s at 9 players). The n = 8 cap happens to equal Squad's pre-8.1 cap.
- **Pixel Ops: lacks.** No first-capture neutral bonus (+20), no enemy-flag capture reward (+50), and no staging period (Squad's is 60–300 s).
- **Pixel Ops: differs (win).** Pixel Ops wins instantly on capturing every location (`aas.js check()`). Squad instead starts a 60/min mercy bleed that the loser can stop by retaking a flag.

### Gaps
- The exact current per-player capture-time table for 1–27 players (after 8.1) and how contested capture scales (difference vs ratio) are not published beyond the three 8.1 data points.
- The "at least three more players" wording on the wiki is ambiguous: it could mean three players minimum or a margin of three over the defenders. I found no patch note that pins it down for AAS/RAAS. Skirmish's per-layer minimum was set to 2 in 2021.

## Key question 3: Random Advance and Secure (RAAS) randomisation

### Takeaway
Current RAAS uses "lanes": each layer defines 1–8 parallel chains (most have 3–5) of 7 flag "positions" (5–6 on small maps). Each position is a cluster of 1–7 alternative capture zones (mean about 2). The game picks a lane and one zone per cluster, so the flag chain differs each round. Unrevealed flags are under fog of war, and capturing a flag reveals the next one. Flag-holding bleed is off; only the 60/min mercy bleed applies. There is no official "RAAS v2" rule set. "RAAS v1/v2/v3" are just layer variants.

### Cited Findings
- "While in AAS the Flag layout is static… in RAAS the Flag layout is randomized and will be different every time you play. In RAAS, teams do not bleed tickets depending on what Flags are captured. Other than that, all the rules and mechanics are identical to AAS" — [Wiki: Random Advance and Secure (edited 2024-09-22)](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Advance_and_Secure)
- Graph structure in layer data 10.0: 35 of 37 RAAS layers use type "RAASLane Graph". Each has `laneObjects` (e.g. Al Basrah RAAS v1 has lanes A–H), each lane running "00-Team1 Main" → "A1…A7-BP_CaptureZoneCluster" → "Team2 Main". Each cluster holds several named candidate capture points; Al Basrah RAAS v1 cluster A2 alone holds 3 ("Airport Hotel", "Terminal Building", …) — [Layer data 10.0](https://github.com/Squad-Wiki/squad-wiki-pipeline-map-data/blob/dev/completed_output/_Current%20Version/finished.json)
- My tally across RAAS layers in layer data 10.0: lanes per layer 3 (15 layers), 5 (10), 4 (6), 1 (2), 7 (1), 8 (1). Flags per lane 7 on 31 layers, 5 on 3 (Kokan, Fool's Road, Chora), 6 on 1 (Logar). Candidate zones per cluster range 1–7, with per-layer means 1.3–4.7. Clusters are almost never shared between lanes (shared clusters: Manicouagan v2 7, Fool's Road 2, Skorpo 1, all others 0). Sumari and Mestia RAAS v1 used an older cluster-only "RAASGraph" with no lanes — [Layer data 10.0](https://github.com/Squad-Wiki/squad-wiki-pipeline-map-data/blob/dev/completed_output/_Current%20Version/finished.json)
- Layer data 10.6: all 40 RAAS layers are "RAASLane Graph". New in 2026: Al Basrah RAAS v3 (1 lane, 7 flags, 3–6 candidates per cluster), Harju RAAS v3_CL (3 lanes × 5 flags), Sanxian RAAS v3_CL (8 lanes × 5 flags) — [Layer data 10.6](https://github.com/aachtenberg/squadmaps-v2/blob/HEAD/data/v10_data.json)
- Patch 8.0 (2024-06-10): "Standardized all large map RAAS layers to have 7 flag layouts. Smaller maps (Chora, Fool's Road, Kokan, Mestia, Sumari, and Logar) still have 5 or 6 flag layers… to standardize the RAAS experience" — [Patch 8.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5746109972564419051)
- Patch 2.7 (2021-05-13): "Updated RAAS Lanes randomization logic, to make sure that the random number generator used is initialized properly… less predictable" — [Patch 2.7](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4767601588111310493)
- Fog of war: Patch 2.15 fixed "RAAS fog of war, which was unintentionally hiding lost and 'double neutral' flags. Now on RAAS map layers, all flags should stay visible for members of a team at all times after they were discovered by that team. A 'lock' icon will appear over a capture zone when a…" (truncated in source) — [Patch 2.15](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4353300134244517023)
- Revealing: Patch 8.1 says faster capture "will allow new strategies for backcapping to unlock/reveal the next flags faster" — [Patch 8.1](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-8-1-release-notes)
- Bleed: Patch 4.3 removed RAAS bleed "on all flags except when all flags have been captured (which still has -60 tickets per minute mercy bleed)… affording teams that have been pushed off a middle flag more time to stabilize" — [Patch 4.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5070528810311080632). Patch 8.1 fixed Belaya RAAS v1 ticket bleed wrongly firing "when the winning team captured the 5th flag" — [Patch 8.1](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-8-1-release-notes)
- Predictability: the community tool Squadlanes offers "Interactive Squad Maps to help with RAAS capture point prediction", with "Capture Points and RAAS lanes… automatically extracted from Squad maps" — [Squadlanes repo README](https://github.com/w4rum/squadlanes). The Squad Wiki points players to Squadlanes and SquadMaps as "a database of all lanes" — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure)
- A popular mod, "Hawks RAAS Rework" ("GRAAS"), "allows for jumping between lanes… makes it impossible to know what the point 2-points ahead will be", i.e. vanilla lanes are disjoint — [The Wrench, July 2021 (OWI)](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4032392655112135400) (cited in OWI's community newsletter; see also [workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2442357787))
- Level design: Manicouagan's points of interest were "pre-planned to improve the balance between having a good variety when random POIs are selected on a game mode like RAAS, while keeping the distances between points roughly fair" — [Manicouagan Map Spotlight (2023-03-13)](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5070528810308215074)

### Inferences
- The data and tooling imply this algorithm: (1) at round start pick one lane at random; (2) for each position along the lane pick one of the cluster's candidate zones; (3) reveal flags progressively (your next attack flag shows once you take your current one), and keep discovered flags visible. I infer this from the graph structure plus Squadlanes' "prediction" purpose and the 8.1 wording. No OWI document spells out the exact lane pick or reveal timing.
- Squadlanes-style tools let players narrow the lane after the first couple of flags, because lanes rarely share clusters. Shared clusters (as on Manicouagan v2) or cross-lane links are the lever for more unpredictability.
- **Pixel Ops: lacks.** No RAAS. On a 1 km map a cheap version would be 2–3 lanes × 5 positions × 2–3 candidate spots each, with fog over unrevealed positions. This also makes the CPU's job harder, because it must scout to learn the next flag.

### Gaps
- Not documented officially: whether the lane is chosen per round or per team, whether the second team's chain is the same lane mirrored, and the exact reveal trigger (on neutralise vs on full capture).
- I found no evidence of an official "RAAS v2" rule redesign or lane-graph rework distinct from layer versions. OWI's Sept 2026 note only says it will be "looking into reworking… AAS/RAAS".

## Key question 4: Invasion

### Takeaway
Invasion is asymmetric. Defenders start owning every flag (now standardised to a maximum of 5, often with 2–5 alternative lanes and about 2 candidate zones per flag) with 800 tickets. Attackers start with 250 and gain +125 per capture. Only attackers can capture, captured flags cannot be retaken, and there is no ticket bleed. Defenders lose no tickets per flag lost until the final flag, which costs them all their remaining tickets. Defenders get early forward infantry spawns.

### Cited Findings
- "The defending team begins with all control points under their control. The attacking team must capture all the flags, in an order that is marked by lattice markers… When the attacking team captures a control point in Invasion, the defending team cannot recapture it." "Capturing… is only possible for the attacking team. There is no ticket bleed for either Defender or Attacker." "In Invasion there is no ticket loss for losing a control point, except the final control point. The Defending team will lose all remaining tickets if they lose their final control point" (section titled "Ticket Values in V13", an old heading) — [Wiki: Invasion (edited 2024-04-16)](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion)
- Tickets now: 250 attackers / 800 defenders on 44 of 45 layers (900 on Lashkar Invasion v1) — [Layer data 10.6](https://github.com/aachtenberg/squadmaps-v2/blob/HEAD/data/v10_data.json); history: 200/800 unified in [Patch 7.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5679673637671132649), attackers to 250 in [Patch 8.1](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-8-1-release-notes). Patch 7.2.1 fixed a bug "that caused ticket numbers to be flipped, resulting in attackers getting 800 tickets and defenders getting 200" — [Patch 7.2.1](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5686430301718695907)
- Capture reward: wiki "+100 for the attacking team that successfully captures a control point" (old) — [Wiki: Invasion](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion). Patch 8.1 "Increased objective capture ticket gain to 130 (up from 100)"; Patch 8.2 (2024-12-02) "Adjusted the number of tickets gained when capturing a flag on invasion from 130 to 125" — [Patch 8.1](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-8-1-release-notes); [Patch 8.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1784506359151949). No later change was found in 9.0–10.6 notes.
- Layout standardisation (Patch 8.2): "All invasion layers will now have 5 flag layouts. The first 2-3 objectives are generally closer together to reduce instances where attackers need to maneuver the entire team and set up new FOBs… The 4th and 5th objectives will generally be more challenging to attack. Most invasion layers will no longer have forward vehicle spawns for defenders and will only have infantry spawns closer to the first objective. Some invasion layers will now have multiple potential lanes to increase replayability" — [Patch 8.2](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1784506359151949). Patch 8.1 had begun "a standard max 5 flag layout" and standardised capture zones — [Patch 8.1](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-8-1-release-notes)
- Graph in layer data 10.0 (type "Invasion Random Graph"): 22 of 38 layers have a single 5-flag lane; the rest have 2–5 lanes (e.g. 3 lanes × 5 flags on 8 layers). Each flag position is a cluster averaging 2.0 candidate zones (max 5), and there are no cross-lane links — [Layer data 10.0](https://github.com/Squad-Wiki/squad-wiki-pipeline-map-data/blob/dev/completed_output/_Current%20Version/finished.json). Capture-zone median sphere radius is 80 m (see Key question 2).
- Forward spawns: "temporary spawns for the defending team… active from the start of the round but are temporary. Time differences vary from layer to layer… Once the time limit is reached, Forward Spawns will disappear" — [Wiki: Invasion](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion)
- Invasion layers can use "single-use" vehicles (no respawn), shown greyed in the command menu — [Patch 2.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4060529371699511300). Amphibious invasion layers (landing ships, AFU 35th Marine Brigade) exist — [Patch 10.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1815580768405314); [Patch 10.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1824644522848347)
- Staging-time server setting does not apply to Invasion — [Patch 10.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1824644522848347)

### Inferences
- Ticket arithmetic: attackers effectively get 250 + 4 × 125 = 750 tickets over the first four captures, close to the defenders' 800. The design pays the attacker per objective rather than making the defender pay, which keeps defenders' tickets as a soft clock on the attackers.
- **Pixel Ops: already have (core) / differs (economy).** Defenders own all locations and attackers take them in order with no bleed (`invasion.js`), which matches Squad. But Pixel Ops gives attackers 650 vs defenders 400 and makes defenders pay 30 per lost location. Squad does the reverse: attackers 250 vs 800, attackers +125 per capture, defenders pay only on the final flag (all tickets).
- **Pixel Ops: differs (clock).** Pixel Ops has a 20-min clock and defenders win at time-out. Squad Invasion has no documented round timer, and defenders win by draining the attacker's tickets.
- **Pixel Ops: lacks.** No randomised Invasion lanes or candidate zones per flag, no early defender forward spawns with a timer, and no no-recapture rule (check: Pixel Ops `capturable()` would let defenders retake a location whose neighbour they still hold).

### Gaps
- No current source states an Invasion round timer, or the forward-spawn durations per layer.
- The "defenders lose all remaining tickets on the final flag" rule is from an old wiki section. It is not restated in 2024–2026 patch notes, though nothing contradicts it.

## Key question 5: Territory Control, Insurgency, Destruction, Skirmish, Seed, Track Attack and other modes

### Takeaway
Current vanilla modes are AAS, RAAS, Invasion, TC, Insurgency, Destruction, Skirmish (reworked in 9.0 into an anchor-plus-five conquest mode with bleed) and Seed. Fireteam (5-player PvE co-op, 10.1) was added in Dec 2025. Track Attack (a 4v4 vehicle duel) existed in 2020–21 but has no layers in the current data. TC is a hex-adjacency mode with a steep graduated bleed. Insurgency and Destruction are attacker-vs-1000-ticket-defender cache hunts with round timers.

### Cited Findings

#### Territory Control (TC)
- In-game description: "teams capture contiguous hex tiles… Teams are unaware of their opponents progress until they capture a tile bordering a tile captured by the opposing team. The team which controls the majority of the map the longest will eventually win" — [Wiki: Territory Control](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Territory_Control)
- Win: "either a) have the other team run out of tickets or b) capture 95% of all hexes"; "games tend to be very long and drawn out" — [Wiki: Territory Control](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Territory_Control)
- Rules (wiki): neutral hexes adjacent to yours "only require one player to capture"; "Enemy hexes need at minimum 3 players or one more than the enemy"; the "Anchor Hex" starts each team's chain and hexes must stay connected to it; "Capturing the enemies Anchor Hex will prevent the enemy team from taking new territory and contesting any hex"; cut-off hexes "cannot be recaptured by friendly players until they are reconnected" and friendlies cannot stop their capture; "An enemy hex takes 45 seconds to neutralized and a neutral hex takes 40 seconds to capture"; "Capturing a hex unlocks all adjacent hex zones"; "Placing a HAB in a hex… will lock it, preventing the enemy… from neutralizing the hex until the HAB is removed"; bleed curve as in Key question 1 — [Wiki: Territory Control (edited 2024-06-10)](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Territory_Control). ### Inferences
- Track Attack appears to be absent from the current vanilla rotation; it survives only in history and mods. I infer this from its absence in both 10.x layer datasets.
- TC's bleed curve is its design core. It is gentle until a team holds 85% (30/min), then brutal (120/min at 90%+), which ends lopsided games before the 95% win line.
- **Pixel Ops: differs (TC).** Pixel Ops has hex adjacency capture, but a flat 12/min majority bleed, an 80% win line, no anchor hexes, no cut-off/reconnection rule, no HAB lock and no fog of enemy progress (`territory.js`). Squad uses a graduated 55%→90% bleed curve, a 95% win line and 400 tickets.
- **Pixel Ops: differs (Destruction).** Pixel Ops has 3 caches, one at a time, revealed by proximity (70 m) or after 90 s, -80 to defenders per cache, a 20-min clock, and 600 vs 450 tickets. Squad has 2–3 phases × 2 simultaneous zones with 4–6 hidden candidate spots each, explosives-only kills, +50 attacker / -10 defender per phase, +33 min clock per phase, 3-min inter-phase delay with temporary defender spawns, and 200 vs 1000 tickets.
- **Pixel Ops: differs completely (Skirmish).** Pixel Ops Skirmish is an objective-less ticket deathmatch (`skirmish.js`). Squad's Skirmish is a 20–30 min anchor-plus-five conquest with majority bleed and an anchor-triggered mercy bleed, which would map well onto a 1 km map with 5 locations.
- **Pixel Ops: lacks** Insurgency (intel-driven cache reveal, 2 active caches), Seed (multiplayer-only, not relevant for single player), Track Attack (could be a vehicle-duel scenario) and Fireteam PvE (Pixel Ops is already single-player vs CPU).

### Gaps
- No current source gives Skirmish bleed rates per point, the TC win threshold re-confirmed after 2024, or Insurgency's current ticket reward and penalty per cache and its round timer length.
- The wiki's TC capture times (45 s / 40 s) and the 2.15 TC curve (60–30 s) disagree, and no newer TC capture data was found.
- The status of the announced TC rework after early 2026 is unknown.

## Key question 6: Match length and what ends a match

### Takeaway
In AAS, RAAS, Invasion and TC the match ends when one side's tickets hit 0. Flags drive tickets, and a full flag sweep only starts a mercy bleed rather than ending the round instantly. Timers are documented for Destruction (time added per phase), Insurgency (insurgents can "wait out the clock") and the old Track Attack (10 min). Skirmish has a 20–30 min target length. I found no hard round timer for AAS, RAAS or Invasion in official sources. Staging lasts 60–300 s (default 3 min).

### Cited Findings
- AAS/RAAS: "The team who runs out of tickets first loses" — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure). Full sweep → mercy bleed "60 Tickets over 60 seconds", stopping if a flag is retaken — [Wiki: Advance and Secure](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Advance_and_Secure); RAAS "-60 tickets per minute mercy bleed" — [Patch 4.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/5070528810311080632)
- Invasion: attackers must "capture all flags and drain the enemy tickets"; defenders "must defend their flags and drain the enemy tickets"; defenders lose all remaining tickets on losing the final flag — [Wiki: Invasion](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion)
- TC: tickets to 0 or 95% of hexes — [Wiki: Territory Control](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Territory_Control)
- Destruction: "Attackers win by completing all phases within the allocated time. Either team loses if they run out of tickets" — [Wiki: Destruction](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Destruction); "roundTimerIncrease" 1980 s per phase, 180 s phase delay — [Layer data 10.0](https://github.com/Squad-Wiki/squad-wiki-pipeline-map-data/blob/dev/completed_output/_Current%20Version/finished.json)
- Insurgency: insurgents win by draining BLUFOR tickets "or run down the in-game round timer" — [Wiki: Insurgency](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Insurgency)
- Skirmish: "target duration of 20 to 30 minutes" — [Patch 9.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1809235871756104)
- Track Attack (historical): "10-minute rounds" — [Patch 1.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/3715990047691621885)
- Staging: default 3-minute staging in some modes — [Wiki: Game Modes](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Modes); server-configurable 60–300 s for AAS/RAAS/TC (PrepTimeStandard) and Skirmish (PrepTimeSmallScale) since 10.3 — [Patch 10.3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1824644522848347)
- End-of-round logging reports winner and remaining tickets, e.g. "Team 2… has won the match with 350 Tickets on layer Anvil AAS v2" — [Patch 3.3 (2022-09-27)](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/4589754330750987945)
- A competitive community mod sets "a match length of 62 minutes, of which 2 minutes are a staging phase" for its layers — [The Wrench Spring 2026 Part 2 (OWI, 2026-04-03)](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/393380/view/1828894815560881) (mod-specific, not vanilla)

### Inferences
- Squad's AAS/RAAS pacing comes from the ticket pool (250) plus objective swings, not a clock. A designer wanting a bounded single-player match can either keep a clock (as Pixel Ops does) or rely on a Squad-style mercy bleed after a full sweep.
- **Pixel Ops: differs.** Pixel Ops uses a 25-min clock (20 min for Invasion and Destruction) and "higher tickets at full time wins" (`rules.js`, `base.js`, `skirmish.js`), plus an instant win on capturing all locations. Squad AAS/RAAS end only at 0 tickets, and a sweep starts a 60/min mercy bleed.
- **Pixel Ops: lacks.** No pre-match staging phase (Squad: 60–300 s, no leaving main, no firing).

### Gaps
- I found no official statement of a maximum match duration (or its absence) for AAS, RAAS, Invasion or TC, nor the default Insurgency and Destruction base timers. Destruction's base timer is not in the layer data I parsed; only the per-phase +1980 s is.
- Typical real-world match lengths are only anecdotal. I found no telemetry or official average.

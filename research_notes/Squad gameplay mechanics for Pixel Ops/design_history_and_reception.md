Research date: 2026-10-25. Each claim is labelled by the kind of source behind it: **[DEV]** = Offworld Industries (OWI) statement or patch notes; **[REVIEW]** = critic or press; **[COMMUNITY]** = player sentiment (Steam threads, fan wikis and guides). ## 1. What OWI and reviewers describe as Squad's core loop and design pillars

### Takeaway
Squad's stated pillar is that coordination should beat individual skill: "Communicate, Coordinate, Conquer". The loop runs through squad leaders who build supplied FOBs and spawn points, a logistics chain that feeds them, and a ticket economy that punishes deaths, lost vehicles, lost FOBs and lost objectives. Reviewers agree the game "only works" when players act as a team, and that the quality of squad leaders makes or breaks a match.

### Cited Findings
- **[DEV/reference]** Squad's motto is "Communicate, Coordinate, Conquer". It is built around strategic teamwork, communication and combined arms in long, large-scale battles, on maps up to 36 km² with MBTs, APCs, trucks and helicopters. — [Wikipedia: Squad (video game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_(video_game))
- **[Reference]** Teams have 50 players, split into squads of at most 9. The squad leader talks to other squad leaders, claims vehicles, builds FOBs and places spawn points. Any squad leader can nominate themselves as commander. Once voted in, the commander coordinates the battle plan and can call UAV reconnaissance and artillery. All kits carry an entrenching tool for building the structures squad leaders place. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_(video_game))
- **[Reference]** FOBs are team-wide respawn points that must be kept supplied through a logistics network. Tickets are lost for losing strategic locations, FOBs and vehicles, and for each infantry respawn. A team whose ticket pool reaches zero loses in any mode. There are seven modes: AAS, RAAS, Invasion (the attacker wins outright on capturing the final point), Skirmish, Destruction, Territory Control and Insurgency. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_(video_game))
- **[Reference, older build]** FOBs can be built only 400 m apart. A FOB becomes active only after a couple of minutes unless a logistics vehicle has dropped supplies. Squad leaders place frameworks for deployables, which other players build with entrenching tools. (These details may come from an alpha build.) — [Giant Bomb: Squad](https://www.giantbomb.com/squad/3030-48072/)
- **[DEV, June 8 2023]** OWI said Squad had drifted from its Project Reality roots, where teamwork mattered most. It blamed shooter trends and solo-friendly design that favoured individual skill, and set out a "see-think-speak-do" philosophy. Problem statement: "a lone wolf flanker can single-handedly decimate an entire squad." Design rule: "speed and aggression" should not be "more effective than communication and coordination." OWI also said "Slightly slower movement creates more time to react and coordinate in combat," and that slower pacing would reduce the squad leader's cognitive load and make vehicles more useful. — [OWI Dev Blog: Infantry Combat Overhaul](https://joinsquad.com/archive/infantry-combat-overhaul-10b70)
- **[DEV, Sept 4 2025]** Update 9.0 added end-of-round Commendations in categories such as Leadership, Teacher and Teamwork, which build visible ranks over time. Stated goal: "Encourage communication, cooperation, and reward positive social interaction." — [Squad 9.0 Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-9-0-release-notes)
- **[REVIEW]** PC Gamer (score 75/100): "The most important thing to understand is that this is a game that only works—and, indeed, is only fun—when you act as part of a team." The review also notes that identifying enemies is harder than in most online games (search summary, page not opened). — [PC Gamer: Squad review](https://www.pcgamer.com/squad-review/)
- **[REVIEW, early access]** PC Gamer's early-access piece describes squad leaders planting rally points as temporary, disposable respawns, and says a squad is only as good as its leadership, which varied widely from game to game (search summary). — [PC Gamer: Squad is an exceptional military shooter so far](https://pcgamer.com/squad-is-an-exceptional-military-shooter-so-far)
- **[REVIEW]** Gamereactor says in-game leaders pushed back hard on lone-wolf play, and that players outside your squad may not revive you (search summary). — [Gamereactor: Squad review](https://embed.gamereactor.eu/squad-review/)
- **[REVIEW]** GamePressure's impressions piece, on Squad as Project Reality's successor: without a squad leader's rally point, players must walk a long way to reach the fight (search summary). — [GamePressure: Impressions of Squad](https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/impressions-of-squad-project-realitys-successor-holds-its-own-und/z97b)
- **[COMMUNITY guide]** One fan guide describes a spawn hierarchy of HAB, then FOB radio, then main base. The HAB holds more spawn capacity than a radio but costs more supplies. A FOB needs logistics-truck access, and building one without a supplied logi truck is a common mistake. — [GuildOrder: Squad FOB and Rally Point System](https://guildorder.com/games/squad/wiki/fob-and-rally-system)

### Inferences
- The loop is a chain of dependencies: **logistics truck → FOB radio → HAB spawn plus deployables → squads fight near objectives → tickets.** Rallies are a cheap, short-lived bridge across the gap between a HAB and the fight. Each link is a different player's job, and most of Squad's friction comes from the hand-offs between them.
- **Pixel Ops relevance:** In Pixel Ops one player commands every squad, so the coordination cost that Squad deliberately creates (talking, agreeing, waiting for a logi driver) disappears. What remains worth copying is the set of spatial and timing decisions: where to put forward spawns, how to keep them supplied, when to commit vehicles, and whether a spawn is worth defending. The communication pillar does not carry over to single-player. The decisions it forces do.
- Pixel Ops already models FOBs as supply-truck builds, tickets and respawn waves, so it already has the backbone. What it lacks is the two-tier spawn system: a durable, supply-dependent team spawn (FOB/HAB) plus a cheap, temporary, squad-only spawn (rally). That pairing is what gives Squad pushes their rhythm.

### Gaps
- I could not open the full PC Gamer review to get its date, reviewer or comments on logistics and the commander. I found no Rock Paper Shotgun review.
- I found no official OWI "design pillars" document beyond the motto and the ICO blog's teamwork framing.

## 2. Major gameplay updates, their rationale and how players received them (dated)

### Takeaway
OWI's big changes since 2021 have tuned spawn pressure, pace and vehicle density: HAB activation delays and enemy-proximity rules (v2.0 to v4.2), the Infantry Combat Overhaul (v6.0, Sept 2023), fewer heavy vehicles and a reworked 20–30 minute Skirmish (v9.0/UE5, Sept 2025), squad-size-based vehicle claiming (v9.6), a PvE bot mode (Fireteam, v10.1, Dec 2025), a second suppression rework (10.5.2, Aug 2026), and a HAB ammo-crate convenience change that was reverted within two weeks (10.6 to 10.6.3, Oct 2026). The ICO split the community. The UE5 move triggered a public trust crisis in Nov 2025.

### Cited Findings

**Commander introduction (Version/Alpha 17; exact date not confirmed)**
- **[REVIEW/press]** Version 17 brought the commander, drones and artillery. The commander is elected from the team's squad leaders and works from a command map with extra tools. UAVs have limited duration, insurgent and militia factions get cruder man-portable quadrotors, and conventional forces get 150mm artillery with adjustable type, angle and spread (search summary, page returned 403). — [Prima Games: Version 17 brings commanders, drones, artillery](https://primagames.com/gaming/squad-version-17-brings-commanders-drones-artillery-to-hardcore-fps)

**Full release (v1.0), 23 Sept 2020** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_(video_game))

**v2.0 (Feb 2021): HAB activation delay**
- **[DEV]** v2.0 added a "HAB Spawn Activation Delay" timer that runs before a new HAB can be spawned on. It started at 10 s and is shown as a circular progress bar on the HAB icon. A later update raised it to 20 s (search summary of v2.0 notes, page not opened). — [Squad Wiki: Update to v2.0](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/WP_-_Update_to_v2.0)
- **[DEV, unverified wording]** One search summary attributes this rationale to the v2.0 notes: reduce "teleportation hopping" onto new HABs, encourage planning, and make HAB/FOB defence about "positioning and area control rather than spawn intensity." - **[DEV, 27 Dec 2021 recap]** v2.0 (Feb 2021) also brought balance and helicopter flight-model changes. — [Steam announcements feed](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?feed=steam_community_announcements&appids=393380&enddate=1642100647)

**v2.9 to v2.11 (June–Aug 2021): HAB proximity-disable rework**
- **[DEV]** v2.9 (late June 2021) made the range at which enemies disable a HAB scale with the number of enemies nearby: up to 80 m, where it had been a fixed 30 m. At least two enemies are now needed to "proxy" a HAB. OWI said the feature was "released prematurely" and that tuning was still being evaluated (search summary of an OWI forum post, page not reachable). — [Squad Forums: Recently released HAB meta changes in v2.9](https://forums.joinsquad.com/discussion/293/recently-released-hab-meta-changes-in-v2-9/)
- **[DEV]** v2.10 (first week of July 2021) addressed issues from v2.9. v2.11 (18 Aug 2021) said OWI was "taking on feedback since our last update regarding the changes to spawn mechanics" at HABs and FOBs. v2.11 also made the FOB exclusion radius configurable per layer (cut from 400 m to 300 m on several Skirmish layers and on Chora, Fools Road, Kokan, Logar and Sumari), and removed or moved defender initial forward spawns on many Invasion layers. — [Steam announcements feed](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?feed=steam_community_announcements&appids=393380&enddate=1642100647)
- **[DEV, v2.11]** Ticket loss for light and medium APCs dropped from 10 to 5. Incapacitated revive times are no longer shortened by small-arms hits, to "emphasize the threat that these larger vehicle/emplaced weapon systems pose to infantry." — [Steam announcements feed](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?feed=steam_community_announcements&appids=393380&enddate=1642100647)
- **[DEV, 9 Dec 2021, v2.12 playtest]** Capture speed now scales with the number of players in the zone, and "buddy boost" was added. — [Steam announcements feed](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?feed=steam_community_announcements&appids=393380&enddate=1642100647)
- **[COMMUNITY wiki, current values unverified]** One wiki gives today's rule as a HAB disabled from up to 90 m by 9 enemy infantry, with each additional enemy adding 10 m, and the spawn reactivating 30 s after enemies leave or are killed or incapacitated. One Steam user says scaling proximity was added after players "farmed" HABs with full squads (search summary). — [Squad Wiki: HAB](https://squad.fandom.com/wiki/HAB); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/3728449612307554250)

**v4.2 (7 Feb 2023)**
- **[DEV]** "Increased HAB spawn activation delay from 20 to 30 seconds." Also fixed enemy Territory Control zones becoming uncapturable after a friendly HAB was placed inside them. No rationale given. — [Squad Update V4.2 Release Notes](https://joinsquad.com/archive/squad-update-v4-2-release-notes)

**Infantry Combat Overhaul, v6.0 (announced 8 June 2023; dev update 20 Sept 2023; released ~26 Sept 2023)**
- **[DEV]** Goals: longer firefights ("create longer firefights and provide more opportunities for tactical choices as a squad"), with fights no longer ending in "one or two shots". Suppression becomes central and gives machine guns a purpose. Slower movement, stamina changes, no mid-air vaulting, and reworked fall damage and leaning. Picture-in-picture scopes trade close-range performance for zoom, so squads benefit from mixed loadouts. Obstacles and chokepoints become meaningful. — [OWI: Infantry Combat Overhaul](https://joinsquad.com/archive/infantry-combat-overhaul-10b70)
- **[DEV]** Suppression rework: stronger weapons create "a more intense, and longer-lasting suppression effect". Suppression lowers combat effectiveness, pushing players to move or take cover. Friendly gunfire does not suppress teammates, though explosives do. — [Squad Update 6.0 Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/archive/squad-update-6-0-release-notes-eb2b4)
- **[DEV, 20 Sept 2023]** Playtest feedback was "overwhelmingly positive", with record public-test participation and more back-and-forth in RAAS and AAS. Newer players found it easier to contribute by laying suppressive fire where teammates directed. Players disliked the initial optic blur and unpredictable sway-based ADS poses, which were cut. A new hurt-state stamina rule (revived players regenerate stamina more slowly) "makes medics more relevant". OWI said it would trade some realism for approachability: "we cannot recreate the fear one experiences when being suppressed by gunfire in real life." — [OWI: Revisiting the Infantry Combat Overhaul](https://www.joinsquad.com/archive/revisiting-the-infantry-combat-overhaul-c0926)
- **[COMMUNITY, 27 Sept 2023]** In a Steam thread the day after release, critics called sway "overkill even in full stamina", the G3 "the most unplayable weapon", and the suppression blur intrusive, and said the game felt sluggish. Supporters said it "was never intended to be run and gun CoD style", and that "Infantry alone is now too slow and unprecise over longer distance to do much on its own" so infantry and vehicles must coordinate. — [Steam: "After the Tactical Squad 6.0 update, what the hell did they do to infantry?"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/3884974596969191428)
- **[COMMUNITY]** Another user says the ICO turned recent reviews "mixed for awhile" and that later tuning made guns more controllable. Defenders framed it as a return to "traditional PR style gameplay" (search summary of Steam threads, single-user claims). — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/0/4354494864105269748); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/4040355128963366606)

**v7.0 to v8.x (2023–2024)**
- **[DEV]** v7.0 added the Turkish Land Forces faction, cosmetic weapon skins and emotes. The official page shows 3 July 2024, which may be a re-post date. — [Squad 7.0 Live & Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-7-0-live-release-notes)

**v9.0, the UE5 transition (3–4 Sept 2025)**
- **[DEV]** Squad moved to Unreal Engine 5.5. Skirmish is no longer a small AAS but a 50v50 infantry mode with a 20–30 minute target length: one anchor point, five points capturable in any order, ticket bleed for holding the majority, and heavy reliance on rallies, with a light vehicle and transport truck spawning after a delay. Capturing an enemy-held AAS/RAAS objective now gives 50 tickets (was 60), neutral flags still 20. Most Combined Arms battlegroups drop from 3 IFVs to 2, to "reduce the overall amount of heavy vehicles within a round of gameplay". Initial spawn delays are now 6–10 min for helicopters and 15–20 min for MBTs. Invasion capture zones were standardised on nine maps. The Unarmed role was removed. Scouts get destructible recon drones. Unfinished FOB deployables refund some resources. Vehicles were retuned toward real top speeds (wheeled faster on roads, tracked better off-road). — [Squad 9.0 Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-9-0-release-notes)
- **[DEV]** 9.0 bot improvements: bots path around deployables, drive vehicles, man turrets, aim at tyres and tracks, take cover to reload, suppress suspected positions, pre-emptively switch to AT weapons when they hear vehicles, are less likely to notice crouched or prone players, cannot see through thick foliage or smoke, and take half the suppression they did before. — [Squad 9.0 Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-9-0-release-notes)
- **[REVIEW/press, 4 Sept 2025]** UE5 was "giving fans with older PCs a tough time" (search summary). — [GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/companies/offworld-industries/z91b42)
- **[DEV + press, 3 Nov 2025]** OWI acknowledged that players feel "feedback is ignored, playtests are disregarded, updates are rushed". Dustin "SgtRoss" Ross: "trust has been shaken, and that's on us." Executive producer Peter Maurice said OWI had "lost sight" of the core community, and promised a player survey, regular "smaller, meaningful updates", and a three-month vehicle-handling roadmap with community playtests. Recent Steam reviews fell to Mixed, while the overall score stayed 84% Very Positive. — [PCGamesN: Squad admits "trust has been shaken"](https://www.pcgamesn.com/squad/trust-shaken); [Allkeyshop](https://www.allkeyshop.com/blog/squad-offworld-admits-breaking-player-trust-promises-changes-news-d/)

### Inferences
- **Spawn pressure is OWI's most re-tuned knob.** HAB activation went 10 s, then 20 s, then 30 s. Proximity disable went from a fixed 30 m, to scaling up to 80 m, to (per the wiki) scaling from 90 m. The FOB exclusion radius was made per-layer. The ammo-crate HAB was added and then reverted. Repeated reversals on the same few numbers suggest players treat these values as central to how matches feel.
- **Pace was slowed on purpose to make coordination pay off.** Slower movement, longer firefights and more suppression went in at v6.0, and fewer, later-arriving heavy vehicles at v9.0. The 10.5.2 change then narrowed suppression so that only real threats suppress, which suggests the 6.0 version was too broad.
- **Pixel Ops relevance:**
  - (a) Keep spawn-denial rules simple, legible and tunable: N enemies within R metres disables a spawn, plus an activation delay after building or after the threat clears. Show the disable radius top-down.
  - (b) Gate heavy assets by time and count, as 9.0 does with 6–10 min helicopter and 15–20 min MBT delays, to shape the early game.
  - (c) The 9.0 ticket reward (50 per enemy-held objective, 20 per neutral) is a useful reference ratio for Pixel Ops' AAS.
  - (d) Squad's bot fairness rules are directly reusable for the CPU: a 150 ms reaction floor, reduced detection of prone and crouched units, no sight through smoke or foliage, and taking suppression.
  - (e) The vehicle-claim rule (unlocks at 2/4/6/8 squad members) is a human-team allocation mechanism. In single-player it maps to "vehicle slots per squad size" or simply a per-team vehicle pool.

## 3. Common player complaints and praise: tickets, spawning, logistics, RAAS, match length, commander

### Takeaway
Players praise the teamwork, the tension and how distinctive Squad's combined-arms play is. The recurring frustrations sit around the coordination layer: no one wants to squad-lead, squad-leader quality varies, spawns are wrong or missing so players face long walks, HABs are camped or "farmed", RAAS objectives feel luck-based, onboarding is steep, and since UE5, performance and trust in OWI. OWI's own March 2024 admissions (commander needs a rework, new-player pain points, ticket losses need explaining) confirm several of these.

### Cited Findings
- **[COMMUNITY] Squad leader burden:** A Steam poster describes offering the squad-leader slot repeatedly with no takers. Another argues squad leading is exhausting and that a commander could take tasks off squad leaders (search summary). — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/3071999401484924438); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/0/2765630416824873519)
- **[REVIEW] Leadership variance:** A squad is only as good as its leadership, and leader quality varied widely from game to game. — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/squad-is-an-exceptional-military-shooter-so-far)
- **[COMMUNITY guide] Spawn frustrations:** One fan guide lists random respawns that scatter the squad, wrong spawns that send players on a long walk to the objective, rallies placed mid-fight that are destroyed quickly, and conflicts between squad leaders and fireteam leaders over rally placement. — [GuildOrder FOB and rally guide](https://guildorder.com/games/squad/wiki/fob-and-rally-system)
- **[COMMUNITY] Logistics and walk-back:** A 2018 thread complains that spawning with little ammo and having to walk back made long rounds even longer (search summary). — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/598515152383446992)
- **[COMMUNITY] HAB "farming":** Players say scaling proximity disable was added because full squads were farming HABs (anecdotal). — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/3728449612307554250)
- **[COMMUNITY] RAAS (Steam thread, 13 Aug 2020):** The original poster finds RAAS frustrating and outcomes luck-based, says squads cannot plan without visible objectives, and proposes revealing friendly objectives first and enemy ones after captures. Defenders prefer RAAS because AAS "always used the same forward bases and buildings" and is a rush mode where losing the first wave decides the round, "which is why most servers run RAAS." Others note RAAS is not truly random: layouts repeat per layer, so veterans learn them, though big maps such as Yehorivka and Gorodok have too many possibilities to memorise. — [Steam: "What is the meaning of RAAS mode"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/4009846212728766520)
- **[COMMUNITY] RAAS skill gap:** Some players say RAAS favours experienced squad leaders who know the lane databases (SquadLanes, SquadMaps) (search summary). — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/3005549744935936042/?l=japanese)
- **[DEV] Tickets opaque:** OWI planned post-game feedback showing "where ticket losses and gains were", which implies players could not see why tickets moved. — [March 2024 Q&A](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-developer-q-a---march-2024)
- **[DEV] Commander:** OWI wants to "completely rework the current commander system up to a higher standard." — [March 2024 Q&A](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-developer-q-a---march-2024)
- **[COMMUNITY guide, unverified]** One guide says the commander is in voice contact with all 5–6 squad leaders at once and gives artillery cooldowns of about 8–10 minutes. It also claims commanders raise win rates by 5–10%, which is unsourced. — [GuildOrder commander strategy](https://guildorder.com/games/squad/guides/commander-strategy)
- **[DEV] Match length:** The 9.0 Skirmish rework set a "target length of 20 to 30 minutes" as a shorter alternative. Wikipedia describes standard play as "long, large-scale battles". — [Squad 9.0 notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-9-0-release-notes); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_(video_game))
- **[COMMUNITY] Onboarding and casualisation tension:** A November 2023 post says getting into the game takes experience that is hard to come by. The 10.6 HAB ammo crate was aimed at "newer players and Squad Leaders" and was reverted. — [March 2024 Q&A](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-developer-q-a---march-2024); [Hotfix 10.6.3](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-hotfix-10-6-3-release-notes)

### Inferences
- Most complaints are about **dead time and blame**, not about the mechanics themselves: walking because no one built a spawn, waiting for a logi driver, a squad leader who places rallies badly, a commander nobody takes. The underlying mechanics (FOBs, HABs, rallies, overrun) are what players credit for the tension.
- **Pixel Ops relevance:** With one human commanding everything, the "someone else failed" frustration disappears. The tedium does not disappear on its own. Keep walking cost meaningful but short on a 1 km map, and let the player fix a bad spawn situation quickly (for example, a rally as an instant stopgap) rather than waiting.
- RAAS randomness reads as unfair mainly when objectives are hidden or unknown. A single-player RAAS could randomise lanes per match but show both teams' current and next objectives. The CPU must never get information the player lacks.
- The ticket-transparency gap OWI admitted to is cheap to fix in Pixel Ops: an after-action ticket breakdown (deaths, vehicles, FOBs, flags) helps players understand losses to a CPU.

## 4. Project Reality heritage: which systems came from PR and how they differ

### Takeaway
Squad was founded by Project Reality developers as PR's spiritual successor, and inherited PR's core: no spawning on the squad leader or captured flags, squad-specific rally points, team-wide FOBs that need supply crates and can be overrun, and a squad-leader and commander voice hierarchy. OWI explicitly framed the 2023 ICO as pulling Squad back toward PR's teamwork-first roots after it drifted toward faster, solo-friendly play.

### Cited Findings
- **[Reference]** Development was announced in October 2014, when Sniperdog (Will Stahl) posted on the Project Reality forums with a 15-person team building a spiritual successor in UE4. The Kickstarter followed in May 2015 (over $200k in five days), Early Access on 15 Dec 2015, and full release on 23 Sept 2020. Many gameplay elements carry over from PR, especially the emphasis on team coordination. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_(video_game))
- **[Reference, PR]** Unlike vanilla BF2, PR players can no longer spawn on their squad leader or on captured flags. A squad leader can place a rally point almost anywhere, and it disappears when enemies are nearby. FOBs are unique to PR: a squad leader deploys one and the squad builds it, it needs supply crates nearby, and the whole team can spawn there unless it is overrun or damaged. A global squad-leader voice channel links all squad leaders and the commander (search summary). — [Giant Bomb: Project Reality: BF2](https://www.giantbomb.com/games/3030-49117/)
- **[DEV]** OWI's ICO blog: Squad had drifted from its PR roots, where teamwork mattered most. The overhaul was "the first step" in realigning all systems with the original vision. — [OWI: Infantry Combat Overhaul](https://joinsquad.com/archive/infantry-combat-overhaul-10b70); [Revisiting the ICO](https://www.joinsquad.com/archive/revisiting-the-infantry-combat-overhaul-c0926)
- **[COMMUNITY]** Players defending the ICO said the devs "decided to return to more traditional PR style gameplay," and that PR "was modded to make teamwork… the most important aspect of the game" (search summary). — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/393380/discussions/6/4040355128963366606)
- **[Reference]** Squad differs from PR in having no hit markers or enemy identification, real ballistics, ever-present friendly fire, and teammates marked on the map (search summary). — [GamePressure impressions](https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/impressions-of-squad-project-realitys-successor-holds-its-own-und/z97b)

### Inferences
- What Squad changed relative to PR, based on the material above: it split team spawning into a FOB radio plus a separate HAB structure; added explicit timers and enemy-count scaling to overrun (v2.0, v2.9); added the commander late (Version 17) with asset call-ins (UAV, artillery) rather than PR's mostly coordinating role; and later added non-PR conveniences (commendations, Skirmish, Fireteam PvE).
- **Pixel Ops relevance:** The PR-era rules are the minimal, proven set: no spawning on the leader or on flags, a squad-only rally that dies to enemy proximity, and a team FOB that needs supplies and can be overrun. Squad's additions are mostly tuning around human behaviour (camping, hopping, hand-offs). A single-player game can adopt the PR core and borrow only the Squad tuning that fixes a problem it actually has, such as the activation delay to stop instant reinforcement.

### Gaps
- I could not access PR's own manual or wiki (realitymod.com) for exact PR rally rules (for example, the minimum number of squad members near the squad leader) or PR's commander assets. The Giant Bomb facts come from search summaries.
- I found no source that lays out Squad-vs-PR differences directly. The differences above are inferred.

## 5. Comparable games, and how these mechanics work against AI or in single-player form

### Takeaway
Hell Let Loose splits Squad's two spawn tiers into Garrisons (team-wide, cost supplies, 200 m spacing, friendly territory) and Outposts (free, squad-only, fast wave), and gives its commander supply drops and vehicle spawns. Foxhole puts the whole supply chain in players' hands, which led to a logistics-player strike over tedium. Post Scriptum/Squad 44 shows that harsher spawn rules can empty servers. Squad's own AI work (9.0 bot improvements, Fireteam PvE, training-bot reaction delays) gives concrete fairness rules for a CPU opponent. I found no top-down tactical game explicitly modelled on Squad.

### Cited Findings
- **[COMMUNITY guides] Hell Let Loose:**
  - Squad leaders place Garrisons and Outposts, and the commander can also place Garrisons. Garrisons need 50 supplies, must be in friendly territory, and must be 200 m from another Garrison. — [Windows Central HLL guide](https://www.windowscentral.com/hell-let-loose-guide)
  - An Outpost is a squad's fast forward spawn: it costs no supplies, has the shortest redeploy wave of the normal spawn structures, and only squad members can spawn there. — [Whisper of the House: HLL Vietnam spawn guide](https://www.whisperofthehouse.com/hell-let-loose-vietnam/spawn-garrison-outpost-guide)
  - The commander can reinforce points and spawn tanks and supply trucks. Supplies come from the Support role's 50-supply box, a commander drop of 100 that lands slowly and is visible to the enemy, and supply trucks carrying 2 crates of 150. The commander's main duty is to watch the map and order squads into position. — [GuildOrder HLL commander guide](https://guildorder.com/th/games/hell_let_loose/guides/commander-role-manpower-and-logistics)
- **[COMMUNITY] HLL squad-leader shortage:** Players wait 20–30 s at match start for someone to take squad leader, and the stress of managing random players drives people away from the role (search summary). — [Steam HLL thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/686810/discussions/0/594021909912392283)
- **[Reference/press] Foxhole:** Designer Mark Ng: "The entire supply chain that drives the war effort is also in the hands of players." In late 2021 and early 2022 the player group L.O.G.I. campaigned for logistics reform over issues such as long pull times from stockpiles and refineries and no closed-loop container logistics, then staged a logistics strike. — [Wikipedia: Foxhole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foxhole_(video_game)); [PC Gamer: Foxhole logistics strike](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/logistics-players-in-mmo-wargame-foxhole-go-on-strike/)
- **[COMMUNITY] Post Scriptum/Squad 44:** Commenters blame FOB and radio spawn changes for pushing long-time players away, leaving newer players unable to hold spawn points and servers dead. Others say FOB radios felt pointless on S44's smaller maps (search summary). — [Steam S44 thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/736220/discussions/0/4348859098835576234)
- **[DEV] Squad bots (9.0):** pathing around deployables, driving vehicles, manning turrets, cover-seeking reloads, suppressing suspected positions, switching to AT when vehicles are heard, reduced detection of prone and crouched targets, no sight through smoke or foliage, half the previous suppression taken. — [Squad 9.0 Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-9-0-release-notes)
- **[DEV] Fireteam (10.1):** Short missions (Combined Arms, Destroy, Extraction, Vehicle Hunt) built on "believable enemy bot behaviours", intended as both a PvE product and an on-ramp to 50v50. — [OWI Fireteam blog](https://joinsquad.com/updates/fireteam-a-brand-new-pve-game-mode-for-squad)
- **[DEV] Bot fairness (10.6):** a 150 ms sight reaction delay "so bots never instantly kill a player before they can reasonably react." — [Squad 10.6 Release Notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-10-6-release-notes)
- **[DEV] Rally-only play needed logistics:** 9.0 Skirmish "relies heavily on rally points". By 10.6, OWI added logistics vehicles "so teams no longer have to exclusively rely on Rally Points." — [9.0 notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-9-0-release-notes); [10.6 notes](https://www.joinsquad.com/updates/squad-10-6-release-notes)

### Inferences (with consolidated Pixel Ops lessons)
- **Notably, OWI's PvE answer is short missions, not a bot-filled 50v50.** Fireteam strips the logistics, FOB and commander layer down to mission-scale objectives. This suggests the full FOB economy is hard to make AI-driven, or is simply not what PvE players want. Pixel Ops is trying something Squad has not shipped: a CPU that runs the whole logistics and spawn economy. The CPU's handling of FOBs, supply runs and spawn denial is therefore where Pixel Ops is unproven and should spend testing effort.
- **What matters most for single-player vs. CPU (ranked):**
  1. **Two-tier forward spawns.** Keep FOBs (durable, supply-fed, team-wide) and add a rally-like squad spawn (free or cheap, temporary, cooldown, dies to enemy proximity). This mirrors HLL's Garrison/Outpost split and PR's FOB/rally split. It is the clearest gap in Pixel Ops' current feature list. Squad's Skirmish shows rally-only play needs logistics as a backstop.
  2. **Spawn denial (overrun) as a readable rule with an activation delay.** Squad's repeated tuning shows it is high-impact. Implement it as one rule with a visible radius, apply it equally to the CPU, and teach the CPU both to hunt player FOBs and to clear threats from its own.
  3. **Logistics as a decision, not a chore.** Foxhole's strike and Squad's "walk back for ammo" complaints show that hauling tedium alienates players. One player managing all trucks needs automation such as standing supply orders, with the interesting choice being where to route supply and when to risk trucks.
  4. **Commander assets as the player's own toolkit.** OWI calls its commander system below standard, and HLL's commander is mostly a supply and vehicle dispatcher. In Pixel Ops the player already is the commander, so add a few cooldown-gated call-ins (UAV reveal, artillery, supply drop) with counterplay (HLL's visible drops) and give the CPU the same toolkit.
  5. **Wounded state plus revive economics.** The ICO made medics matter through hurt-state stamina after revive. v2.11 made revive timers resistant to small-arms hits. A wounded state with a bleed-out timer creates rescue-or-push decisions that a single player can manage at squad level.
  6. **RAAS-style lane randomisation with full objective visibility,** to get RAAS replay variety without the "luck-based, can't plan" complaint.
  7. **Pacing gates on heavy assets** (9.0's helicopter and MBT delays) and **ticket transparency** (OWI's planned ticket breakdown).
  8. **CPU fairness rules** taken directly from Squad's bot work: a reaction-time floor, reduced detection of prone units, smoke and foliage blocking sight, and taking suppression. Squad's later suppression narrowing (errant rounds do not suppress, faster decay) is a caution against over-broad suppression in Pixel Ops' existing system.
- **What to simplify or drop:** vehicle-claim rules based on squad size, commander elections, entrenching-tool "shoveling" labour (OWI's own 10.6 rationale treats shoveling as friction), wave-timer desync edge cases, and per-server voting and rotation rules. These exist to manage human players and add little against a CPU.
